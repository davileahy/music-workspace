# Music Workspace

## Vision

Music Workspace is an open-source platform designed to help musicians organize, study, and play music in one place.

Instead of focusing only on displaying chord sheets, the platform provides a complete workspace where users can manage their personal music library, study chords, practice songs, and use tools that improve their playing experience.

The long-term goal is to become the best place for musicians to keep and interact with their music collection.

---

## Problem

Musicians often rely on multiple disconnected tools to study and play songs.

A typical workflow may involve:

- Chord websites
- PDFs
- Images
- Notes applications
- Messaging apps
- YouTube or streaming services

This creates a fragmented experience filled with duplicated content, advertisements, context switching, and poor organization.

---

## Solution

Music Workspace centralizes everything related to studying and playing music into a single application.

The platform combines:

- A public music library
- A personal music workspace
- Chord diagrams
- Music organization
- Practice tools
- Community-driven improvements (future)

The application should prioritize simplicity, speed and organization above feature quantity.

---

## Target Audience

Music Workspace is built primarily for musicians who want to learn, organize, practice and play songs.

The initial audience includes:

- Guitar players
- Acoustic guitar players

Future versions may support additional instruments without changing the core experience.

---

## Product Principles

Every feature should reinforce these principles.

### Organization first

Music should be easy to find, organize and revisit.

### Simplicity over complexity

The interface should remain clean and intuitive.

### Fast by default

Common tasks should require as few interactions as possible.

### User ownership

Users should own and organize their personal workspace.

### Open source

The project should remain transparent, community-driven and extensible.

---

## Goals

The project aims to:

- Provide a modern music workspace.
- Offer an excellent user experience.
- Centralize music-related tools.
- Reduce dependence on multiple websites.
- Enable community contributions in the future.
- Serve as a high-quality open-source project.

---

## Non-Goals

The project is **not** intended to:

- Replace professional DAWs.
- Become a music streaming service.
- Prioritize monetization over user experience.
- Add features that increase complexity without solving real problems.

---

## Long-term Vision

Music Workspace should evolve into the primary workspace musicians use before, during and after playing music.

Whether the user is:

- studying a song,
- importing a chord sheet,
- creating original music,
- organizing playlists,
- taking notes,
- practicing chords,

everything should happen inside a single application.

The platform should grow incrementally while remaining simple, fast and enjoyable to use.
